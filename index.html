Privacy Policy

App: Claude Automate (Meta App ID 1631282508071267) · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

Claude Automate is a private, internal tool operated by HR SAP LD, Mauritius. It manages the operator's own Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and Meta ad accounts. It is not offered to the public and is not used to manage other people's or other businesses' accounts.

2. What the app does

  • Creates, edits and reports on ads in the operator's own ad accounts.
  • Reads and replies to Messenger conversations that customers start with the operator's Pages.
  • Reads Page and ad performance statistics.

3. Data we process

  • Messenger conversations: your public Facebook name, Page-scoped ID and the messages you send to our Pages. These are used only to answer your enquiry, process your order and provide after-sales support.
  • Order details: the product you ordered, payment confirmation and delivery information you send us.
  • Advertising data: aggregated, anonymous ad statistics (reach, clicks, cost) provided by Meta. We do not receive personal data about people who see our ads.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for their own marketing. Data may be processed by service providers that run the app on our behalf (hosting and AI text-processing services), only to provide the service to you.

4. How long we keep data

Conversation and order records are kept for as long as needed to provide support and warranty service, and to meet accounting and legal obligations (normally up to 7 years for transaction records), then deleted.

5. Your rights

Under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

6. Data deletion instructions

To have your data deleted:

  1. Send a message to the Facebook Page you contacted with the words "Delete my data".
  2. We will delete your conversation and order records held by the app within 30 days and confirm by message, except records we must keep by law (for example invoices), which are kept only for the legally required period.

You can also remove the app's access at any time from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, or delete the conversation in Messenger.

7. Security

Access tokens and records are stored on access-controlled systems and are never published. Only the operator has access.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

9. Contact

HR SAP LD, Mauritius. To contact us, send a message on Messenger to the Facebook Page you dealt with.
